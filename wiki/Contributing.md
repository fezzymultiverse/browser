# Contributing

Short version: small merge requests, bump `version` in `package.json`, update `CHANGELOG.md` and `HANDOFF.md`, never commit keys, and say what you tested. Full rules are in CONTRIBUTING.md in the repository, and conduct rules in CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md.

Use the issue templates for bugs and feature requests. Security problems go in a **confidential** issue (see [[Security Model]]).
