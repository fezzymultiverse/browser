# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We want Festers Browser to be a friendly, harassment-free project for everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Expected behaviour
- Be respectful and patient, especially with people new to Android, Capacitor or Git.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Describe what you tested and what you did not.

## Unacceptable behaviour
- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Spam, or pushing people to run code you have not explained.
- Anything else that would be out of place in a professional setting.

## Scope
This applies to issues, merge requests, wiki pages, comments and any other space for this project.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues or merge requests, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through the GitLab profile of the project owner. Reports are handled with discretion.
